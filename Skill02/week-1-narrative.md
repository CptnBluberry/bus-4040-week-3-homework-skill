## Slide 4 - Why is a Business School Teaching an AI Course

- Anyone paying attention to the modern world recognizes AI as a unilateral game change
- Until recently, the bottleneck in building software were expert human software engineers
- The person with the business problem described it roughly, handed it off, and hoped for an acceptable outcome
- AI is removing this bottleneck and shifting the work "left"
- This course explore cutting edge techniques business are using to shift software development towards business leaders and Product owners
